How TechEpoch works

TechEpoch is an automated tech news feed. Four times a day, a pipeline collects stories from public, keyless sources, ranks them, and publishes this page. No editors, no accounts, no tracking.

Sources

  • Hacker News — front page plus targeted AI/robotics searches (via the Algolia API)
  • arXiv — newest papers in cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.RO, cs.CV
  • Reddit — r/MachineLearning, r/artificial, r/technology, r/robotics
  • RSS — Google AI Blog, MIT Technology Review, Ars Technica

Ranking

Every story is classified into AI, Robotics, Machine Learning, Research or Tech. AI, Robotics and ML stories are deliberately boosted above general tech, then ranked by keyword relevance, community signal (e.g. HN points) and recency. Duplicates across sources are merged.

Sharing

Every story has a share button: one tap posts to LinkedIn, X, Facebook, WhatsApp or Telegram. For Instagram, the site generates a 1080×1920 story card in your browser with a pre-written caption — download it, share to your story, paste the caption. Instagram offers no web posting API, so this is the same flow every sharing tool uses.

Fair use

We publish headlines, short summaries and links only — full articles live with their publishers. If you run one of these sources and want out, open an issue on the repo.
